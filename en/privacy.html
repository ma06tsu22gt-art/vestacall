VESTA CALL: Space Battleship Battle Privacy Policy

Effective / last updated: November 9, 2026

STEAM BASE ("we") explains below how the game "VESTA CALL: Space Battleship Battle" (the "App") handles information about its users.

1. Information we collect

The App has no account registration or login of our own, and we do not collect your name, phone number, email address or similar information. Your game progress (save data) is stored on your device. If you are signed in to Google Play Games, it is also stored on Google's servers so that you can carry it over (see "3. Cloud save"). We do not operate any server that receives save data, and save data is never sent to us. If you contact us by email, we use your email address and message only to reply to you and for no other purpose.

3. Cloud save

To let you carry your progress over when you change devices or reinstall, the App uses the "Saved Games" feature of Google Play Games services. Your game progress data (progress, items owned, records of purchases and so on) is stored on Google's servers, linked to your Google account. We do not individually view or use this saved data; it is used only to carry over your progress. If you do not sign in to Play Games, your data is stored only on your device. You can delete Saved Games data from the Google Play Games settings. For how Google handles data, see the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Advertising

The App currently shows no ads and does not use the advertising ID. If we add ads, we will update this page to let you know.

5. How we use information

We use only aggregated information from these services (such as revenue and stability) to operate and improve the App. We do not provide your personal information to third parties except as required by law.

6. Children

The App is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. The age-based monthly purchase limits shown in the Legal Notices are a safeguard in case the App is used by someone under 18.

7. Security

Communication with these services is encrypted (HTTPS).

8. Deletion

Save data on your device is deleted when you uninstall the App (or clear the App's data in your device settings). Cloud save data can be deleted from the Google Play Games settings. Purchase records are held by Google Play and can be checked in your Google account's purchase history. For information held by the third-party services, you may request deletion under their policies. To request deletion of emails you sent us, contact us at the address below.

9. Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page.
