VESTA CALL: Space Battleship Battle Privacy Policy

Effective / last updated: November 8, 2026

STEAM BASE ("we") explains below how the game "VESTA CALL: Space Battleship Battle" (the "App") handles information about its users.

1. Information we collect

The App has no account registration or login of our own, and we do not collect your name, phone number, email address or similar information. Your game progress (save data) is stored only on your device and is not sent to our servers (we do not operate any server that receives save data). If you contact us by email, we use your email address and message only to reply to you and for no other purpose.

3. Advertising

The App currently shows no ads and does not use the advertising ID. If we add ads, we will update this page to let you know.

4. How we use information

We use only aggregated information from these services (such as revenue and stability) to operate and improve the App. We do not provide your personal information to third parties except as required by law.

5. Children

The App is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. The age-based monthly purchase limits shown in the Legal Notices are a safeguard in case the App is used by someone under 18.

6. Security

Communication with these services is encrypted (HTTPS).

7. Deletion

Save data exists only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App (or clear the App's data in your device settings). Purchase records are held by Google Play and can be checked in your Google account's purchase history. For information held by the third-party services, you may request deletion under their policies. To request deletion of emails you sent us, contact us at the address below.

8. Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page.
